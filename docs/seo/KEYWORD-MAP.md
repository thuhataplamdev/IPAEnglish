## 4. Mapping rules

1. Do not exceed 20 approved keywords in this engagement.
2. Map every approved keyword to one of the 4 target URLs above.
3. Group close variants by intent instead of creating competing landing pages.
4. Avoid assigning the same primary intent to multiple target URLs unless there is a clear business reason.
5. Titles and headings must remain natural; do not force exact-match keyword repetition.
6. Existing content may be adjusted, but full new-article/page writing is outside scope.
7. Week 1 GSC data may reorder P1/P2 priority or replace a weak term with another term, but the total remains capped at 20 and any URL-map change must be recorded.
8. English-language counterparts are technical parity checks only and are not additional keyword targets in this engagement.
